# MED-04 — source-bound informational schedule persistence

Implemented owner-only recording, correction and retirement under the approved medication/source lifecycle, with immutable versions and medication-equivalent patient/source View for reads. Schedule state is separate from treatment status. Medication/source changes require explicit owner review before a previously recorded schedule can be used again; restore or source re-review alone never reactivates it.

Each version binds to an exact medication reconciliation entry/revision. New recordings/corrections require its current reviewed source and explicit informational meaning/confirmation. Owners may retire an existing unchanged definition even when its source requires review: retirement cannot edit the definition, reactivate it or imply stopping treatment. Any later recording requires fresh source-bound confirmation. CAS, canonical semantic retries, actor checks, patient/medication/document locks and atomic value-free audit prevent duplicate or unaudited changes.

Added GET/PUT `:medicationId/schedule` under the existing protected medication controller. Latest/current and twenty-version history pages remain separate. Inventory and schedules share the existing service's locked current-record authorization/final recheck through a narrow internal context; inventory behavior is unchanged. Response guards reject contradictory history/current state. The pure source-match helper is not authorization, a prescription, a date-window decision or reminder dispatch.

Migration25 creates schedules and events with source/owner/revision guards, forced RLS and SELECT/INSERT-only runtime privileges. Literal timing, exact dates, bounded ordered weekdays/times and named-zone validation preserve precision without next-dose or DST instant inference. SQL composes the existing inventory literal validator; no clinical placeholder fields or original/source rewrites. Existing24 migrations are unchanged. Prisma models reflect the new tables.
