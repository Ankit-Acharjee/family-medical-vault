# AUTH-04–AUTH-05 / AUTH-07 — Activation backend foundation

Implements the approved authority: original managed creator with current patient-wide View and Manage issues/revokes and separately approves a verified claimant. Claims from accounts already owning a Person are deferred; no automatic merge, record movement or replacement Person ID. Claiming alone grants no patient-record access or ownership. Explicit creator confirmation uses the exact private claimant reference. Current caregivers remain explicit grantees after activation.

Authenticated creator and claimant routes enforce session, Origin/CSRF, bounded strict bodies and body-only purpose-separated capabilities. Hash-only secrets are issued once; issue retry returns no recovered secret. Require explicit expiry1–168hours, allow one outstanding unexpired link per managed Person, and bound history20. Claimed references are private to the claimant and authorized creator. Inspect creates no identity or grant. Accepted tokens cannot claim again; a current authenticated new owner can obtain the completed operation status without granting new authority.
